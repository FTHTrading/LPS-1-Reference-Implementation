# Chapter 7: The Group Chat from Hell

The climax of the comedy arrived on a Tuesday afternoon.

An absolute donut of an operator in Douala—probably working from a noisy cybercafé off the main avenue—was trying to coordinate the next round of fake documents. He wanted to add the Kenyan broker and the Zambian prince to a group chat to align their stories. 

By mistake, he added Kevan.

The group was titled `NKAMWA / GENESIS / EMERALD TRANSIT`.

Kevan sat in front of his monitor, watching the messages pop up in real time. He didn't say a word. He just opened his screen recorder, grabbed a bag of chips, and watched the show.

`[14:12] Douala Op:` *"Brothers, the white man is asking about the Mumbwa license again. He says the satellite map shows only dirt. John, did you send the updated Chase draft?"*

`[14:15] John Andrew Mwita:` *"I sent the MT760, but his compliance team is asking for the SWIFT transmission logs. We need a fake bank header. Who has the Chase stamp template?"*

`[14:18] Prince Divine:` *"I told you to use the Dubai assay! The Al Sawaya logo always makes them happy. Nguyen, is the Viet-Zam website live? He is checking the registry."*

`[14:20] Nguyen (Vietnam):` *"Yes, but the domain took too long to resolve. I used your recovery Gmail. Did he wire the $28k?"*

`[14:22] John Andrew Mwita:` *"Not yet. He says his wife is arguing with him about the fees. He is very stubborn. We need to send him the customs release certificate with the government seal. Who has the Cameroon Ministry seal?"*

`[14:25] Douala Op:` *"I have it on the old laptop. Let me modify the date. We will send it in one hour. Tell him the port director is threatening to seize the cargo if he doesn't pay today."*

Kevan took a sip of his whiskey, his eyes watering from laughing. 

They were arguing over who was responsible for photoshopping the stamps, using broken English, all while the target of their scam was sitting in the chat, silently recording every message, every phone number, and every voice note.

"This is not a syndicate," Kevan whispered, saving the video file to his secure backup directory. "This is a digital comedy troop."
