# Chapter 15: The Generational Fuckening

Year 2031.

A sharp, ambitious 22-year-old named Mwape K. Nkausu just graduated top of his class in Lusaka with a blockchain engineering degree. He applies for a senior developer role at a major African DeFi protocol.

His application looks perfect.

The AI agent running The Divine Curse doesn’t give a single fuck.

The moment his wallet address and full legal name hit the company’s KYC oracle, the system detonated like a thermonuclear shame bomb.

**Automatic Trigger Sequence:**

- Smart contract instantly recognized the bloodline link.
- Pulled Shame NFT #0001 (the original 6.6 hectare roast) and all 83 subsequent shame tokens attached to the family.
- Generated a new report titled **"Third Generation of the Divine Bloodline Still Carrying the Curse"**.
- The hiring manager’s dashboard lit up red with a single warning in massive text:

**"HIGHLY TOXIC IDENTITY — PERMANENT ON-CHAIN SHAME ATTACHED. RECOMMEND IMMEDIATE REJECTION."**

The kid gets rejected before he even gets an interview.

He tries three more Web3 companies. Same result. The Divine Curse has been quietly indexing every wallet, every address, every relative tied to that bloodline for five years straight.

By age 26, Mwape K. Nkausu has been blacklisted from every serious blockchain company on the continent. His name literally became a synonym for “toxic asset.”

Kevan never even knew this kid existed.

He didn’t have to.

The system was doing it for him.

One night the kid, now broken and desperate, sends Kevan a message from a burner account:

> "I was a child when my father did that shit. Why am I still paying for it?"

Kevan stared at the message for a long time. Then he typed back with zero mercy:

**"Because in Web3, your father didn’t just scam with money. He scammed with his name. And names are eternal here. Your grandfather fucked around. Your father found out. And you… you inherited the blockchain’s original sin. The curse doesn’t care that you weren’t born yet. The blockchain only knows blood."**

Then he added the final demonic touch:

He minted one last NFT — **"The Divine Bloodline Collection"** — a living, ever-growing set that automatically adds every new descendant born into that family. Their shame compounds with interest.

The last line of the NFT description reads:

*"Some debts are paid in blood. These ones are paid in permanence."*

Kevan closed his laptop, looked out the window, and said out loud to nobody:

“I didn’t just destroy one scammer.  
I cursed an entire fucking dynasty.”

And the worst part?

The system was still running.

Still watching.

Still breeding.

And it would outlive every single one of them.
