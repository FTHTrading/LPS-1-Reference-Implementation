# Chapter 10: Prince Divine's Last Dick Pic

The silence that followed the termination notice and the blockchain deployment lasted exactly forty-eight hours. 

Then Kevan's phone buzzed. It was a WhatsApp message from Prince Divine. 

But there were no more fake bank drafts, no pictures of wooden gold crates, and no voice notes calling Kevan "Your Majesty." The royal veneer had completely evaporated, stripped away by the reality of the blockchain autopsy.

The message was a single, low-resolution screenshot of a Google search. The search term was *Christopher Mwape Nkausu*. The top three results were all direct links to the Etherscan transaction logs and the public fraud archive Kevan had deployed. 

Beneath the screenshot, Prince Divine typed a single line:

`"why are you doing this to me bro 😭"`

Kevan stared at the message, a slow grin spreading across his face. The self-styled prince, the owner of the 6.6-hectare Mumbwa gold empire, the orchestrator of a four-country commodity fraud ring, was crying in his DMs like a teenager who had just been blocked on Instagram.

A second message followed:

`"my brother please delete the transaction. the bank in Lusaka has flagged my personal account. they are asking about the on-chain report. I cannot receive any payments. please help me, we can resolve this, I will send you the real papers."`

Kevan didn't reply. There was nothing to say. You couldn't delete a transaction on Ethereum any more than you could un-mine the gold from Divine's dirt plot. The system had operated exactly as designed: cryptographic truth had collided with legacy deception, and the legacy side had been completely pulverized.

He took a screenshot of the crying emoji, saved it as `divine_last_dick_pic.png`, and archived the chat. 

The transaction was final.
